# Decision Log

Every locked-in design, governance, architectural, art, and tooling decision is recorded here so any human or AI tool knows **what** was decided and **why**.

---

## Decisions

| ID | Date | Category | Decision | Rationale / Notes | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D-001** | 2026-11-18 | Vision | **3D Reboot (Fresh Repo):** Create a new 3D game in `dungeon-crawler-3d` rather than extending the 2D prototypes (`Dungeon-Crawler-Game-2`, `dungeon-crawler-engine`). | Carry forward concepts and lessons learned without inheriting 2D engine constraints. | Locked |
| **D-002** | 2026-11-18 | Gameplay | **Skyrim North Star:** Gameplay feel is guided by **Skyrim** above all other references. | Primary compass for camera, exploration, combat weight, and world immersion. | Locked |
| **D-003** | 2026-11-18 | Tech | **Leverage Existing Open-Source Engines/Libraries:** Do not build a 3D engine from scratch; maximize use of free, open-source engines and libraries while keeping full flexibility. | Avoids months of low-level engine bugs and lets us focus on gameplay and art. | Locked |
| **D-004** | 2026-11-18 | Licensing | **100% Free & Open Source:** All engines, libraries, tools, and project output must be completely free and open source. | Zero licensing fees, royalties, or proprietary lock-in. | Locked |
| **D-005** | 2026-11-18 | Art | **100% In-House Original Art (Blender + Open-Source Tools):** All game art is created by us; no third-party asset packs. | Full ownership, cohesive art direction, no redistribution restrictions. | Locked |
| **D-006** | 2026-11-18 | Gameplay | **Floor Collapse Timer = 5 In-Game Days (~30 min/day initially):** Each floor collapses after 5 in-game days (each day ~30 real-time minutes for now, configurable). | Creates sustained exploration pressure without rushing the player like a 5-minute arcade timer. | Locked |
| **D-007** | 2026-11-18 | Gameplay | **Floor Collapse = Reset to Floor Start (No Permadeath):** When a floor collapses, the player resets to the start of that floor instead of losing the whole run. | Keeps collapse meaningful without punishing multi-hour runs with full wipes. Exact keep/lose rules to be refined in Round 2. | Locked |
| **D-008** | 2026-11-18 | Gameplay | **No Staircase Previews:** Descending to a new floor does not preview the upcoming floor's theme or contents. | Preserves mystery and discovery when entering a new floor. | Locked |
| **D-009** | 2026-11-18 | Quality | **Automated-Testing First & Multi-AI Repo Standard:** Automate everything testable to minimize manual user testing; maintain universal AI docs (`AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, etc.) and changelogs for everything. | Reduces user testing overhead and guarantees seamless handoff across any AI tool. | Locked |
| **D-010** | 2026-11-18 | Governance | **Roles & Autonomy Split:** User = Product Owner & Creative Director; AI = Full Technical Lead, Engineer & Technical Artist with full autonomy over code, organization, libraries, testing/CI, docs/changelogs, logged minor balance tuning, and first-pass Blender assets. AI asks only for core gameplay/north-star changes, art direction/key model approvals, scope changes, and major irreversible tech/platform choices. | Keeps the User focused solely on creative direction and feedback while AI owns full technical quality and proactive push-back. | Locked |
| **D-011** | 2026-11-18 | Workflow | **PR-per-Milestone & Batched Review Cadence:** Once implementation starts, work in feature branches, open a PR per milestone/phase with plain-English summaries, and wait for User approval to merge. User playtests only at meaningful playable milestones and reviews Blender art via batched preview renders. | Prevents review fatigue and keeps manual testing minimal while preserving User sign-off on meaningful milestones. | Locked |
| **D-012** | 2026-11-18 | Gameplay | **Camera Perspective:** First-person primary with an optional toggle to over-the-shoulder third-person (Skyrim-style). | Maximizes immersion while honoring the Skyrim north star. | Locked |
| **D-013** | 2026-11-18 | Gameplay | **Core Combat Foundation:** Left-hand/right-hand equipping (sword+shield, dual-wield, sword+spell, two-handed), weighted melee swings/power attacks/blocking/stamina, magicka spellcasting (offense, healing, buffs), and archery/ranged weapons with manual crosshair aiming. Stealth/sneak-attacks deferred out of core foundation. | Captures Skyrim's signature combat variety while keeping core scope focused. | Locked |
| **D-014** | 2026-11-18 | Gameplay | **Floor-Entry Snapshot on Collapse:** On floor collapse, the player keeps the character level and gear they entered the floor with, and the floor resets fresh with a new 5-day timer. | Creates genuine stakes for each floor attempt without erasing multi-floor run progress. | Locked |
| **D-015** | 2026-11-18 | World | **Floor Structure = Massive Interconnected Dungeon Labyrinth:** Each floor is a sprawling interconnected labyrinth of dungeon rooms, halls, and corridors (like a giant Skyrim Nordic ruin). | Fits both *Dungeon Crawler Carl*'s dungeon premise and modular 3D Blender dungeon-kit architecture. | Locked |
| **D-016** | 2026-11-18 | Gameplay | **Skyrim Exploration Touches:** Top compass bar revealing nearby landmarks as you approach, interactive world looting (chests, enemy bodies, containers, levers, traps), and full time-pause when opening inventory, map, or skill menus. | Delivers unmistakable Skyrim pacing and tactile world interaction without unnecessary subsystems (no lockpicking minigame or outdoor day/night sky cycle required). | Locked |
| **D-017** | 2026-11-18 | Gameplay | **Save-and-Quit Anytime:** Auto-saves exact player position, floor timer, and floor state so sessions can be paused/resumed across multiple sittings, while floor collapse still resets to the floor-entry snapshot. | Essential for 2.5-hour (5 in-game day) floors without breaking floor-collapse stakes. | Locked |
| **D-018** | 2026-11-18 | Gameplay | **Combat Death = Floor-Entry Snapshot Reset:** Dying in combat triggers the same reset as floor collapse — return to the floor-entry snapshot (level/gear from floor entry) with a fresh 5-day timer. | Unified, unambiguous stakes for both combat and exploration without full-run permadeath. | Locked |
| **D-019** | 2026-11-18 | Progression | **Hybrid Progression:** XP from combat, quests, and exploration grants Character Level-Ups + Perk/Skill Tree points, AND using weapons/magic improves their proficiency Skyrim-style. | Blends Skyrim's satisfying "use-to-improve" feel with structured RPG buildcraft. | Locked |
| **D-020** | 2026-11-18 | Loot | **Skyrim Slots + Chaotic Sponsored/Mythic Affixes:** Equipment uses Skyrim-style slots (weapons, shield, armor, rings, amulets) powered by rarity-scaled affixes and wild Sponsored/Mythic game-changing effects. | Combines familiar Skyrim gearing with exciting *Dungeon Crawler Carl*–style loot highs. | Locked |
| **D-021** | 2026-11-18 | World | **Discoverable Safe Rooms & Underground Towns:** Hidden throughout the labyrinth where enemies cannot enter, featuring Vendors, Quest Boards, and Stash/Rest spots. | Rewards exploration and creates dramatic relief pockets inside a hostile 5-day labyrinth. | Locked |
| **D-022** | 2026-11-18 | World | **Hidden Staircase + Optional Sealed Boss Arenas:** Exploring reveals the hidden exit staircase; optional sealed Boss Arenas guard high-tier Treasure Vaults. | Creates tense risk-vs-time decisions: push for boss vault loot or descend before collapse. | Locked |
| **D-023** | 2026-11-18 | Tone | **Full "Dark Baseline, Goofy Payload":** Grim, atmospheric Skyrim-like dungeon visuals and deadly enemies contrasted with a snarky Dungeon AI, absurd achievement popups, and ridiculous sponsored loot. | Delivers the unmistakable *Dungeon Crawler Carl* identity over a serious Skyrim-like dungeon foundation. | Locked |

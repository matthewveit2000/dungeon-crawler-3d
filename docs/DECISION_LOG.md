| **D-038** | 2026-11-20 | Gameplay / Core Identity | **Core Gimmick = The Power-Scaling Race:** The game's central challenge is whether the player can **power-scale fast enough to survive the end of each floor**. Every floor ramps up in difficulty, so the player must level up heavily and secure strong gear on (and between) floors to avoid falling behind the curve. The player can **always reset their progress to the start of any floor they have reached** (restoring that floor's Floor-Entry Snapshot), letting them go back and rebuild if they fall too far behind. | Gives every floor a clear, compelling goal beyond survival — outgrow the dungeon's escalating difficulty — and turns gear, XP, vendors, bosses and vaults into the heart of the loop. The any-floor reset is the safety valve that keeps a run recoverable without removing the stakes of `D-014`/`D-018`. Implementation details (difficulty curve, reset UI, which snapshots are stored) are still to be designed. | Locked (User directive) |
| **D-039** | 2026-11-20 | Gameplay / Tech | **One Fixed Seed — Every Attempt Replays the Same Floor:** Every playthrough uses the same run seed (`20261118`), and each floor's layout, room dressing, enemy placement, vendor stock **and loot** (chests, crates, urns, enemy drops) are derived from that seed + floor number. Each container and enemy rolls its loot from its own seed, so contents never depend on the order things are opened or fought, and the shared combat/AI random state is reseeded at the start of every floor attempt. Dying or a floor collapse therefore replays an identical floor. | Makes the power-scaling race (`D-038`) learnable: players can memorise a floor, plan the best route to gear, and know exactly what a reset gives them back. Also keeps bug reports and automated tests fully reproducible. | Locked (User directive) |
| **D-040** | 2026-11-20 | Art / Animation | **First-Person Viewmodel Pose System v3 — Weapon-First Keys, Arm IK, Edge-Leading Cuts:** First-person animation is authored as data in `player_controller.gd` (`FP_*` pose library): each key says where the fist is, which way the blade/haft points and which way the cutting edge faces (the direction the cut travels). Each forearm's roll comes from a 2-bone arm solve toward the shoulder, the weapon is rolled inside the grip so its edge always leads, and keys are sampled along Catmull-Rom arcs with per-phase easing (settling wind-up, accelerating strike, bleeding follow-through). The two-handed axe is posed as one object with both fists placed on its leather wraps. Spell arms are turned palm-to-target (the open-hand meshes were authored backwards and mirror-swapped), the bow nocks an arrow only while drawing and draws to the right cheek with the bow upright just left of the crosshair, and a new `fp_upper_arm` sleeve runs from each elbow back to the shoulder so forearms never end in a floating elbow guard. Iterated with `./scripts/fp-pose-probe.sh` (player view + side + top views) and guarded by `test_fp_viewmodel.gd`. | User review: the battleaxe faced sideways, sword swings did not read as cutting away from the player, hands were off, spell palms faced the player and the bow draw looked wrong. Supersedes the first-person part of `D-037`; combat timings (`D-033`) are unchanged. | Locked |
| **D-041** | 2026-11-20 | Visual / Lighting | **Brighter Dungeon Lighting Pass:** Roughly twice as many wall torches (every other wall in rooms and corridors, cap 72 -> 240) that burn brighter (energy 1.55 -> 2.2, range 9.5 -> 11.5 m); fire braziers in the corners of ordinary rooms (2 in small rooms, 4 in larger ones, skipping doorway corners and setpiece rooms); a soft shadowless overhead fill in every room; ambient light 0.22 -> 0.55 (0.35 -> 0.65 on Ultra), warmer ambient tint, exposure 1.0 -> 1.2, depth/volumetric fog roughly halved; the OpenGL fallback may show 16 lights at once (was 6). Shadows stay limited to the nearest torches. | User feedback: the game was too dark. Keeps the warm firelit dark-fantasy mood while making rooms, corridors and enemies readable. | Locked |
| **D-042** | 2026-11-20 | World / Design | **Larger, Denser Floors + Vault Guarantees:** Floors grow from a 48x48 to a 72x72 tile grid with 28-36 rooms (was 14-20, max room size 7 -> 8), more loop corridors (0.28 -> 0.38), about twice the enemies (floor 1: 14 -> 30, floor 2: 18 -> 38), 1-3 crates/urns per combat room, a higher chest chance (0.55 -> 0.6), wall dressing driven by the `ambient_prop_density` setting (now 0.6) and up to 480 torches. On the default seed this means ~2x the walkable area (8.4k -> 17.2k m2), 31 enemies, 17 chests and 45 containers. The Treasure Vault gets its own compass/map landmark, and the game re-opens the vault every frame the boss is dead but the vault is shut, covered by a physical walk-into-the-vault test across four seeds. | User feedback: floors should feel larger and more dense; the boss vault was reported unreachable after the boss fight (not reproducible on current main, so it is now guarded and easier to find). | Locked |
